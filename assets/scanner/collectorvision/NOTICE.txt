The vendored worker and Deckhand's scanner integration are available as source
in the served worker and the application's JavaScript source map.

Inference uses CollectorVision's Fastweb-Single 1.39 and Milo 1.0.0 models and its
live MTG reference catalog from https://github.com/HanClinto/CollectorVisionCatalog.
Deckhand checks for compatible Milo1 catalog updates every six hours.
Recognition is experimental; printing, foil and condition need confirmation.

ONNX Runtime Web 1.24.3 by Microsoft and contributors
https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime
MIT License: ONNXRUNTIME-LICENSE.txt

Reference card images and names belong to their respective rights holders.
Models are served by Deckhand and cached on-device. Only the normalized
128-dimensional Milo1 embedding is sent to Deckhand for catalog lookup.
Photos stay on-device. Scanning requires a connection to Deckhand.
Server catalog client source and license: server/scanner/vendor/NOTICE.txt.
