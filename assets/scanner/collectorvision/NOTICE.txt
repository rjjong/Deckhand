The vendored worker and Deckhand's scanner integration are available as source
in the served worker and the application's JavaScript source map.

Inference uses CollectorVision's Fastweb-Single 1.39 and Milo 1.0.0 models and its
prepared MTG reference catalog dated 2026-07-09. Recognition is experimental;
newer cards may be absent, and printing, foil and condition need confirmation.
